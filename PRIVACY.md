# Privacy Policy for Math Mishaps

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Math Mishaps ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Math Mishaps animation series and associated automated publishing tools. This Privacy Policy explains how our internal application handles user data when interacting with third-party social media platforms (including TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram).

## 2. Information We Collect
The Math Mishaps application only collects and processes authentication tokens and API credentials provided directly by the account owner to publish approved animation videos and retrieve channel performance analytics. We do not collect, sell, or share personal user data with any third parties.

## 3. How We Use Data
We use API access solely for:
- Uploading and publishing owner-approved 2D animation video masters.
- Attaching appropriate video titles, descriptions, and hashtags.
- Monitoring video view counts and basic engagement metrics for channel analytics.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Our services interact with the TikTok Open Platform, YouTube Data API, and Meta Graph API. Use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies.
